// Board of Education

Five elected members serving four-year terms. The Board sets policy, governs the district under state and federal law, and is responsible for its financial stability. Meetings are open to the public and staff are welcome to attend and speak.

6:30 PM on the second Monday of every month — except November, which moves to the third Monday. Held at the Board Office, 1731 Goodman Avenue.
