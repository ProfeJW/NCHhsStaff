Staff Info

Timesheets, payroll & paperwork

// Internal Sub & Detention Forms

Due every week — submit even if you only covered one period, and even in weeks with no days used per the district instructions.

  • Complete the Internal Sub form
  • Complete the Detention form
  • Late forms are processed in the next pay period

// Classified Staff Timesheets

Submitted biweekly, on the 15th and 30th of each month.

  • Place completed forms in Ms. Bratchette’s mailbox
  • Forms received after the cutoff move to the next pay period

// Pay Discrepancies

Notice something off with your pay? Contact payroll@nchcityschools.org right away so it can be resolved before the next cycle.
