The negotiated agreement between the North College Hill Education Association and the Board of Education, effective August 1, 2025 through July 31, 2027. This is the document that governs your workday, your leave and your pay — when a question comes up about any of them, this is the answer.

// Field Trips & Transportation

Buses are booked with the Transportation Request Form. It needs your signature and the principal’s, then goes to Central Office — so start it early rather than the week of the trip.

Transportation Request Form (PDF) Field Trip Form — Clinic

Two forms, not one. The transportation form books the bus. The clinic form tells the nurse who is going, so medications and health needs travel with the group. Submit both.

Before you fill it in

  • Work out your bus count first — 7–12th grade is 48 passengers per bus, and that number excludes teachers and chaperones. (K–6 is 72.)
  • You need two named chaperones on the form.
  • Tick the right budget line. High school: 001-4100-141-021 field trips · 001-4130-141-021 music & band · 001-4510-141-021 sports.
  • You’ll need departure time, return time, and the destination place and town.
  • Transport is run by First Student; the travel certificate at the bottom is completed by the district, not by you.

Per the Master Contract (§ 6.06), students travel in Board vehicles when they are available, and no one can be required to transport students in a personal vehicle. This is the 2025–26 form — check with the office before a 2026–27 trip in case it has been reissued.

// Injury & Incident Reporting

Report every injury or incident — even if you don’t expect a Workers’ Comp claim. The district files a year-end report with the state covering all workplace injuries, so nothing can be skipped. Call 911 first for anything serious or life-threatening.

What to do

  • Tell your supervisor immediately.
  • Complete the First Report of Injury (FROI) — normally within 24 hours.
  • Seek treatment if needed and give the Sedgwick ID card (in the packet) to every provider you see.
  • If you see a doctor at any point within a year, take the Physician’s Report of Work Ability and return it to your building administrator.
  • Have witnesses complete a Statement of Witness. The form says notarised — have them fill it out either way. Extra copies are with your building secretary.
  • Return the packet to the building principal, who forwards it to Central Office Payroll. Keep the physician portion if you haven’t been seen yet.
  • Keep administration updated on your status until Workers’ Comp accepts or denies the claim.

The Supervisor’s Investigation Report is completed by the building administrator, not by you. Questions → your building secretary.

EAP Overview

FMLA

Unpaid, job-protected leave for your own serious health condition or to care for a parent, spouse or child. Leave can be taken all at once or intermittently.

  • Schools are covered regardless of size — the 50-employee threshold doesn’t apply here.
  • You must have worked for the district 12 months (not necessarily consecutive) and 1,250 hours in the past year.
