Inclement weather: Plan A = one-hour delay (day runs 9:05–3:05). Plan B = two-hour delay (see tab above, 10:05–3:05).
On a two-hour delay students should not arrive before 9:50; those arriving 9:50–10:05 report to the cafeteria and may not be elsewhere without a pass.

// Monday Meeting Schedule

Meeting Mondays run 7:35 AM – 3:15 PM, with lunch 11:00–11:30 AM.

The cycle runs on a four-week rotation. The 8:00 and 11:30 blocks change with the week; everything else is the same every Monday.

Week 4Aug 24 · Sep 28 · Nov 9 · Dec 14

First semester only — spring dates are in the teams PDF. Aug 24 is Team Leads & Admin only. No meeting Sept 7 — Labor Day holiday.
