// How This Year Differs

Four board-approved calendars side by side, including next year’s. Most of the year simply shifts a day or two earlier — but two changes are worth planning around.

Third quarter ends over a week earlier, and no longer runs up to spring break. Q3 closes 5 March 2027, against 13 March last year and 14 March the year before. Two full weeks of teaching then follow before the break. If you have paced Q3 to finish the Friday before spring break, that no longer holds — and it is not a one-year quirk: 2027–28 does the same thing, closing Q3 on 3 March with the break three weeks later.
Spring break moves a week later and gains a day on the end. It runs 22–26 March, and the Off Day lands on Monday 29 March immediately after — so the building is closed from Saturday 20 March through Monday 29 March, ten days straight. Last year the Off Day sat alone in April, and it returns to April in 2027–28 — so this stacking is a one-year arrangement.
