// Free Supplies for Your Room

Crayons & Beyond (formerly Crayons2Calculators) runs a Teacher Resource Center where K–12 educators shop for classroom supplies. Registration for 2026–27 is open. There is a $30 access fee for the year — $25 if you register early. Some schools pay the fee for their teachers, so check with the office before you pay it yourself.
