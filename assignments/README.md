# Assignments

Adapted classroom materials. Not linked from the site navigation and not indexed
by `search-index.json` (that index only covers `docs/`).

Nothing here may contain student-identifying information — see the privacy note
in the repository README. These are blank materials only.

## Los Artículos de la Confederación (US History, grade 10)

- `articulos-de-la-confederacion-ELL-worksheet.docx` — student sheet, 3 pages
- `articulos-de-la-confederacion-ELL-answer-key.docx` — answer key plus teacher notes

Written for a native Spanish speaker at a low English proficiency level.
Instructions are in Spanish; the content is in simplified English with inline
Spanish glosses. Roughly 30–35 minutes. 48 points plus a 2-point bonus.

The student sheet opens with three Spanish-language links so the student meets
the content in their stronger language first. **Check the links before
assigning** — they were selected from search results but not opened.
